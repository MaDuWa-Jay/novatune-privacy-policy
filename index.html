About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the NovaTune Android application (NovaTune, package com.brand.novatune). It describes how the app accesses, uses, stores and shares information when you use its music-player features.

Developer / Publisher: MaDuWa
Privacy contact: devnovastudios@gmail.com

NovaTune is designed to play audio stored on your device. The app does not require you to create an account and does not operate a NovaTune account server or cloud music library.

This policy describes the app's current features and the third-party services integrated into it. If the app adds analytics, accounts, cloud services, advertising partners, or other data-processing features, this policy and the app's Google Play Data safety information will be reviewed and updated before the changed version is released.

02

Information handled on your device

When you grant the relevant Android permissions or select an audio file to open, NovaTune accesses information needed to provide music playback and related features.

Music library

Audio and track details

NovaTune may read track title, artist, duration, album information, file name, media identifiers and available album artwork from Android's media library. This information helps display, organize, search and play your songs.

Shared content

Audio opened from another app

If you choose NovaTune to open or share an audio item from another app, Android provides access to the item you selected so NovaTune can play it. NovaTune does not send that audio file to the developer.

App storage

Preferences and playlists

Favorites, custom playlist information, playback and audio-effect preferences, visualizer style and colour choices, appearance settings, and limited ad-frequency settings are stored in the app's private on-device storage.

No NovaTune account

No developer music cloud

NovaTune does not upload your music files, music-library contents, favorites or custom playlists to a NovaTune-operated server in the current version.

Local music processing: NovaTune reads the media information needed to display and play a track. Ordinary playback does not modify your music files. A file is deleted only after you choose the delete action and complete the applicable Android authorization or confirmation flow.

03

Permissions and how they are used

NovaTune requests permissions for specific music-player features. You can review or revoke permissions in Android Settings. If you deny a permission, the feature that needs it may be unavailable.

Permission / accessWhy NovaTune uses it
Music and audio access
READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE		To find and play audio stored on your device and read the track information and artwork needed by the music library. The permission used depends on the Android version.
Legacy storage write access
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE · Android 9 / API 28 and earlier		Used only when you choose to delete an audio file from shared device storage on Android versions that require this legacy permission. Newer Android versions use the applicable system authorization flow.
Microphone / audio permission
RECORD_AUDIO		Required by Android's playback Visualizer API. When you enable the visualizer, NovaTune reads a partial, low-quality representation of the device's audio output mix to draw live visual effects. Because the app uses the output-mix audio session, this mix can include audio playing from other apps at the same time. This is not a microphone voice-recording feature: NovaTune does not intentionally make, save or upload voice recordings or audio samples.
Audio settings
MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS		Used for playback audio features and to create the Android output-mix visualizer. Audio effects such as bass boost and virtualizer are applied to the active NovaTune playback audio session when available.
Display over other apps
SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW		Used only if you enable the optional floating player, so its playback controls can appear over another app. This permission does not give NovaTune a feature to record or read the contents of other apps' screens.
Internet and network state
INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE		Used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to request and display ads and by related Google privacy/consent services. Network state is also checked so ad requests can be retried when a connection becomes available.
Background playback
WAKE_LOCK / foreground media playback service		Used to keep music playback and media controls working while the app is in the background or the screen is off. These permissions do not provide NovaTune with your GPS or precise location.

The reviewed app manifest does not request GPS/location, contacts, camera, or photo/video-library permissions.

04

Advertising and information shared with Google

The current version of NovaTune includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Legacy), version 25.5.0, and displays banner, native, interstitial, app-open and rewarded advertisements. The app also uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to manage applicable consent and privacy messages.

Information the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share

According to Google's disclosure for Google Mobile Ads SDK (Legacy) version 25.5.0, the SDK automatically collects and shares certain information for advertising, analytics, ad measurement and fraud-prevention purposes. Depending on the SDK, device, settings and applicable choices, this may include:

  • IP address: may be used to estimate a device's general location.
  • User product interactions: information about app launches, taps, ad interactions and video views.
  • Diagnostic information: app or SDK performance information, such as app launch time, hangs and energy/performance signals.
  • Device and account identifiers: Android Advertising ID, App Set ID and, where applicable, other identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device.

Google may use this information to deliver advertisements, measure ad performance, manage ad frequency, understand service performance and help detect invalid activity or fraud. Where permitted by applicable law and your settings or consent choices, it may also be used for personalized advertising. The Android Advertising ID is subject to the controls available in Android settings.

Google states that information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Legacy) is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS). Google's handling of information is also governed by its own privacy terms and controls.

Google consent and privacy messages

NovaTune requests updated consent information from Google's UMP SDK and displays a consent form when Google determines that one is required. Depending on the privacy message and applicable region, UMP may also require a privacy-options entry point. When required, a Privacy Choices option is made available in NovaTune Settings; selecting it opens Google's privacy-options form. The availability and choices shown can vary by region and applicable requirements.

UMP is a Google service. It communicates with Google to retrieve consent information and load applicable privacy messages. Consent status and form availability are managed through the UMP SDK; NovaTune does not operate a separate account-based consent database.

Important distinction: The advertising SDK may process device and advertising information as described above. This does not mean NovaTune sends your local music files, track library, favorites or custom playlists to the NovaTune developer. The developer does not receive those local music files or playlists through the app's AdMob integration.

05

When information may be shared

NovaTune does not transmit your local music library, music files, favorites or custom playlists to the developer in the current version. The main third-party data processing in the app is performed by Google's Mobile Ads and UMP services as described in Section 4.

  • Google: The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share the advertising, interaction, diagnostic and device information listed above with Google and relevant service providers for the purposes described in Google's documentation.
  • Apps you choose: If you use Android's Share feature to send an audio item to another app, Android transfers the item you selected to the app you chose. That receiving app handles the item under its own privacy policy.
  • Legal requirements: Information may be disclosed if required by applicable law, regulation, court order or valid legal process.

NovaTune does not sell your local music files, playlists or locally stored app preferences. No NovaTune-operated analytics or cloud music server is included in the reviewed project version.

06

Storage, retention and deletion

  • Music files: Your music files remain in storage locations managed by Android and you. NovaTune does not maintain a separate server copy of your music library.
  • App preferences: Favorites, playlist information and app preferences are kept in NovaTune's private on-device storage while the app is installed. You can clear NovaTune's app storage or uninstall the app to remove locally stored app data.
  • Android backup: Android's backup and device-transfer features may back up or restore eligible app data according to Android's behavior and your device/account settings. NovaTune does not independently access or manage your Google account backups.
  • Deleting a music file: Audio files are deleted only after you choose the delete action and complete the applicable Android permission or system confirmation flow. The exact confirmation steps depend on the Android version.
  • Advertising information: Information collected by Google through its advertising services is retained and managed by Google under Google's policies and settings. NovaTune's developer does not control Google's retention periods.

The current version does not create user accounts, so it does not have a NovaTune account-deletion process. To remove locally stored app data, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall NovaTune. For information held by Google, use Google's privacy and advertising controls.

07

Security

NovaTune uses Android's app permission system and private app storage to protect locally stored app data. The app's reviewed Android configuration disables cleartext network traffic. Google states that data collected by its Google Mobile Ads SDK (Legacy) is encrypted in transit using TLS.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we aim to use platform protections and the security practices provided by the third-party services integrated into the app.

08

Children's privacy

NovaTune is a general-purpose music player and is not specifically designed as a child-directed service. The current version has no NovaTune account system and the developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information through user accounts.

NovaTune is ad-supported. Google advertising services may process device or advertising information as described in this policy, subject to applicable law, consent requirements and service settings. The app's target-audience declaration and advertising settings must be configured accurately by the developer in Google Play Console and AdMob. This policy does not replace those required declarations or settings.

09

Your choices

  • Music and audio access: You can allow or deny music/audio permissions through Android's permission prompts and Settings. You can also revoke access later in Android Settings.
  • Visualizer: You can decline the RECORD_AUDIO permission. Live playback visualizer effects will then be unavailable. You can leave the visualizer screen to stop its audio analysis.
  • Floating player: You can enable or disable the optional overlay through Android's special app access settings.
  • Local app data: You can clear NovaTune's app storage or uninstall the app to remove locally stored preferences and playlists.
  • Advertising choices: When Google's UMP SDK determines that a privacy-options entry point is required, the Privacy Choices option appears in NovaTune Settings. Use it to review or update the privacy choices available in Google's form.
  • Google advertising controls: You can review available ad-personalization and advertising controls through Android settings and Google My Ad Center. Available controls may vary by region, device and Google service.
