1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how NovaTune ("NovaTune", "we", "us", or "our") accesses, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the NovaTune Android music player. It applies to the app package com.brand.novatune.

This policy describes the project version reviewed on November 2, 2026. If the app adds analytics, accounts, cloud services, new advertising partners, or other data-processing features, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration must be updated before that version is released.

2. Information handled on your device

NovaTune is designed to play audio stored on your device. In the reviewed version, the app does not require you to create an account and does not include a NovaTune-operated account server or cloud music library.

  • Music library and metadata: When you grant music/audio access, NovaTune reads audio items available through Android's media library, such as track title, artist, duration, album information, file name, and available album artwork. This lets the app display and search your songs and build playback lists.
  • Shared audio: When you choose NovaTune to open an audio file from another app, NovaTune reads the audio item that Android shares with it so you can play it. The app does not send that audio file to the NovaTune developer.
  • App preferences: Favorites, custom playlist information, playback and equalizer preferences, visualizer style and colour choices, appearance settings, permission choices, and limited ad-frequency settings are stored in the app's private on-device storage.

NovaTune does not upload your music files, music-library contents, favorites, or custom playlists to a NovaTune server in the reviewed version. The app may read the metadata needed to display and play a track. It does not modify your music files during ordinary playback. If you use a delete feature, Android asks you to approve the requested deletion of the selected audio item.

3. Permissions and how they are used

  • Music and audio access (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO on supported Android versions, or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on older versions): used to find and play audio stored on your device and show its metadata.
  • Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): used only if you enable the optional floating player, so its playback controls can appear over another app. The permission itself does not mean NovaTune records what is displayed in other apps.
  • Microphone / audio permission (RECORD_AUDIO): Android requires this permission for the playback visualizer API used by NovaTune. In the reviewed implementation, the visualizer analyses the currently playing device audio locally to draw visual effects. NovaTune does not intentionally record your voice, create microphone recordings, save audio samples, or upload microphone audio. You can decline this permission; live visualizer effects will then be unavailable.
  • Network access (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to request and display advertisements and to support its related services.
  • Playback service and wake lock: used to continue audio playback and maintain playback while the screen is off. These features do not provide NovaTune with your precise location.

NovaTune does not request GPS/location, contacts, camera, photos, or video-library permissions in the reviewed manifest. Your Android settings let you review or revoke app permissions. Some features will not work if their required permission is denied.

4. Advertising and information shared with Google

The reviewed version includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and displays banner, native, interstitial, app-open, and rewarded advertisements. The SDK may automatically collect and share certain information with Google for advertising, ad measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. Depending on the SDK version, features used, device settings, and applicable consent choices, this may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a device's general location;
  • app and advertising interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad/video views;
  • diagnostic and performance information, such as app launch time, hangs, and energy/performance signals;
  • device and account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID, App Set ID, and other applicable identifiers.

Google and its advertising or service partners may use this information to deliver ads, measure ad performance, manage ad frequency, prevent invalid activity and fraud, and, where permitted by applicable law and the user's settings and choices, provide personalised advertising. NovaTune's developer does not receive your local music files or playlists through the AdMob integration.

Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. Google's handling of information is also governed by Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure
  • Google My Ad Center

NovaTune does not sell your local music files, playlists, or locally stored app preferences. The advertising SDK's collection and sharing described above is performed by the third-party advertising service, not by a NovaTune-operated analytics server.

5. When information may be shared

Other than the advertising SDK activity described above, NovaTune does not transmit your local music library or app preferences to the developer in the reviewed version. If you use Android's Share feature to send a track to another app, Android transfers the item you selected to the app you chose. That receiving app handles the item under its own privacy policy. We may also disclose information if required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

6. Storage, retention, and deletion

  • Music files remain in the locations managed by Android and the user. NovaTune does not maintain a separate server copy of your music library.
  • Favorites, playlists, and preferences are kept in the app's private storage on your device while the app is installed. You can clear NovaTune's app storage or uninstall the app to remove locally stored app data. Android device/cloud backup and restore settings may affect whether some app data is copied or restored by Android.
  • Audio files are removed only when you choose an applicable delete action and approve Android's system confirmation.
  • Advertising-related information collected by Google is retained and managed by Google under its policies and settings. NovaTune's developer does not control Google's retention periods.

The reviewed version does not create user accounts, so it does not have a NovaTune account-deletion process. You can remove local app data by clearing storage or uninstalling the app. For data held by Google, use the Google privacy and advertising controls linked above.

7. Security

NovaTune uses Android's app permission and private-storage mechanisms for app data. The reviewed Android configuration disallows cleartext network traffic. Google states that data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's privacy

NovaTune is a general music player and is not specifically designed as a child-directed service. The developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information through a NovaTune account system; the reviewed version has no account system. Advertising services may process device or advertising information as described in this policy. The app's target audience and advertising settings must be configured accurately in Google Play Console and AdMob before release.

9. Your choices

  • You can allow or deny music/audio and visualizer permissions through Android permission prompts and Settings.
  • You can enable or disable the floating player through Android's special app access settings.
  • You can clear NovaTune's app storage or uninstall the app to remove locally stored app preferences and playlists.
  • You can manage available advertising and privacy choices through your Android settings and Google's privacy/ad controls. Choices and controls may vary by region, device, and Google service.
